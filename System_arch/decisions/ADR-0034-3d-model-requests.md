# ADR-0034: 3D model requests (S4)
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-26
- **Affects:** design/19-supabase.md, design/23-email.md, design/15-product-editor.md, design/12-features-admin.md, docs/SECURITY_REVIEW.md, docs/OPERATIONS_RUNBOOK.md, ADR-0021, ADR-0029, ADR-0033
- **Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-26-s4-3d-model-requests-design.md` (the owner's decisions of 2026-10-26 at the end of the spec override the defaults above them)

## Context
The owner wants businesses to get 3D models by asking for them: a business sends photos, the platform team makes the model and uploads it, the business is told when it is done, and the 3D part of the product stays hidden until then. Whether a business may upload its own files is a per-business choice that only the platform makes. There is no AI or automatic 3D generation and no cost per request (hard rules).

## Decision
- **A new platform permission, `models.manage` ("Handle 3D model requests"), against ADR-0029.** ADR-0029 says a permission without a feature does not exist and that new features add their key to the check constraint. This is such a feature: it has its own pages and its own private data (request photos). Reusing `products.edit` would give every default Team member the request photos and the deliver and decline emails, and would stop the Owner from giving a 3D artist only this job. The key needs `products.view` and `businesses.view` (the queue shows product and business names), recorded in `private.permission_prerequisites()` and `PERMISSION_PREREQUISITES`. There are now 16 keys. The Owner has all of them, Admin gets `models.manage` in a backfill (one `access_permissions_changed` audit row, `reason: 'model_requests_backfill'`), and Team does not by default. `admin_set_role_permissions` keeps its own list of known keys, so it is redefined to accept the new one.
- **The switch, enforced by the database.** `businesses.allow_own_models` defaults to `false`. Existing businesses that already have a product or option 3D file start `true`, so nobody loses a capability they use. Only the audited `admin_set_own_models` function changes it (a `BEFORE UPDATE OF` trigger refuses every direct update), and it needs `models.manage`. With the switch off, `can_write_own_models()` is false for the business, and triggers on `product_models` and `product_variant_models`, `save_product` and the `product-media` storage policy refuse GLB and USDZ writes (`own_models_not_allowed`). Deleting a product or an option still cascades. `save_product` leaves model rows alone when the payload's sets equal the stored ones or `keep_models` is set, so a business with the switch off still saves photos, text and prices. Turning the switch off keeps existing files; only staff can change them from then on. Staff with `products.edit` or `models.manage` can always upload.
- **The request lifecycle.** `requested` to `cancelled` (the business, until work starts); `requested` to `in_progress` (staff, by Start work or the first staged file); `requested` or `in_progress` to `rejected` (shown as "Declined", with a required reason that is sent to the business); `in_progress` to `delivered` (needs a staged GLB). The last three states are final, and the business can send a new request for the same target afterwards. A request is for a product or for one of its options, with at most one open request per target (a partial unique index). The owner decided that there is **no limit on open requests per business**: the 25-open rule and its constant are gone. The product needs width, height and depth above zero.
- **A private photo bucket.** Request photos live in `model-request-photos` (private, 10 MB, JPEG, PNG or WebP, 2 to 10 per request), apart from the catalog photos. They are never public and never copied into the gallery; the business and `models.manage` staff read them through signed URLs. They are uploaded on Send, so an abandoned form leaves no files. They are kept after a request ends so the team can revise a model, deleted when a request is cancelled, and removed with the product, the option, the business or the closure sweep.
- **Staging in `product-media`, delivery replaces the whole set.** Staff upload the GLB and USDZ through the shared `ModelSection` (same checks, preview and scale check) into `product-media` at the path shape the model tables already need, and stage them on the request. Staged files are not product files and are not served publicly. `admin_deliver_model_request` replaces the target's whole model set with the staged files in one transaction (a format that is not staged is removed, so a GLB never pairs with an older USDZ of another model), stamps `products.models_changed_at`, and returns only the replaced paths that no product row still uses, for the browser to delete.
- **Hidden until ready.** Public pages show 3D and AR only when a usable model row exists. A product with an open request can be published photo-only, and nothing about the request is public. AR stays native only (ADR-0021); no AR mode changes.
- **A delivery is never undone by an older editor.** The editor sends the `models_changed_at` it loaded; `save_product` refuses a changed model set when the stored value is newer (`models_changed_elsewhere`). Saves that do not change models skip the check.
- **One Edge Function for the staff actions that email.** `review-model-request` (`verify_jwt = true`) runs the 2FA gate, calls the SQL function as the caller, and emails the business owner (kind `model_request_update`, EN or PT, deduped by `email_log.workflow`). An email failure never rolls back the status. A direct SQL call still works and sends no email.
- **Demo mode** follows the same rules with session-only photos and models, labelled, and the toasts say "Demo: no email was sent."

## Consequences
- Staged model files are readable by the business through its own `product-media` folder before delivery (accepted; see the security review).
- Delivery removes a business's own USDZ when staff deliver only a GLB. The deliver dialog says so.
- Email is best effort. The status in the dashboard is the durable record.
- A browser closed mid-send, or a failed clean-up, can leave orphan files. There is no sweeper (a non-goal); partial clean-up is reported.
- The rollback needs the request photos exported and the bucket emptied first (customer data). Delivered models stay as ordinary rows.

## Alternatives rejected
- **Reuse `products.edit`.** It would give the wrong people the request photos and email actions.
- **Hide the upload controls in the UI only.** The business API would still accept a GLB; the database is the boundary.
- **Stage in the private photo bucket.** It needs a cross-bucket copy at delivery; the staged-file exposure is smaller than that complexity.
- **Merge delivered files into the existing set.** A GLB could pair with a USDZ of another model, which is the bug `variantMedia` already avoids.
- **A per-business request limit.** The owner declined it.
