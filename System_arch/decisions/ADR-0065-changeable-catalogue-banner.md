# ADR-0065: Changeable catalogue banner
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-11
- **Affects:** design/14-features-public.md, design/12-features-admin.md, design/19-supabase.md; amends ADR-0062 (not superseded) and extends ADR-0063 (crop on upload)

## Context
The top of a public catalogue (ADR-0062) shows one fixed Sanctus photograph under a dark veil. The owner (2026-11-11, `docs/DECISIONS.md`) wants it changeable: the platform sets a default banner for all catalogues, and each company can upload its own that replaces the default on its catalogue. Only the picture changes ("Picture only"): the eyebrow, company name, logo and piece count stay automatic.

## Decision
- **Order of the picture:** the company's own banner, else the platform's default banner, else the built-in Sanctus photograph (`lamp-vase.webp`, still anchored at `object-[50%_44%]`; an uploaded picture is centred). The choice is one pure function, `chooseCatalogBanner` (`src/features/public/catalogBanner.ts`). While the published site content is still loading and the company has no banner of its own, the band stays plain dark instead of flashing the built-in photograph; if the site content cannot be read, or the default has no address (a demo upload after a reload), the built-in photograph shows.
- **Look:** unchanged except the picture. The dark veil (`bg-obsidian/45`) and the bottom gradient stay on every picture so the white text is readable on any photograph; the band keeps its responsive height (19 / 24 / 30 rem) and the picture covers it (`object-cover`). The frame is `catalogBanner` in `src/lib/imageFrames.ts`: `cover(2 / 1, 'aspect-[2/1]')`. The crop dialog crops to 2:1; because the band is wider or narrower than 2:1 depending on the screen, a narrow screen trims the picture's sides (and a very wide one its top and bottom). Both editors say so, in the wording pattern of the hero note (`editor.crop.bannerNote`, `ui.banner.trimNote`).
- **Platform default:** a "Catalogue banner" panel at the top of the Website Editor's Site tab (above the site icon): Upload (`UploadButton` with the `catalogBanner` frame), Edit picture (`EditPictureButton`, reopens the original with its crop) and Remove (back to the built-in photograph). It is `chrome.catalogBanner` in the site-content document (`{path, originalPath?, crop?}`, the `SiteAsset` of the other uploaded pictures; no description, the picture is decorative). It is part of the draft, goes live with Publish, is counted in the publish summary and in the problems list. The public read returns it as `catalogBanner` in the `chrome` part, so the catalogue gets it from the chrome it already loads.
- **Company banner:** a "Catalogue banner" field next to the logo in the shared company form (`BusinessForm`, new `BannerUpload`), so the company owner (settings, "Business") and the platform admin (company page) both get it, with the permission the logo has. Upload, Edit picture and Remove (Remove = back to the default). The original and the edited copy are both uploaded (same crop dialog and Edit picture behaviour as product photos); Save changes stores them. A company that is not saved yet gets a sentence ("Save the company first") instead of the field: its storage folder is the company id, and a banner (two files and a crop) would need a staging area like the logo's `new/` folder, which is not worth it for a one-time step.
- **Database (migration `catalogue_banner`, not applied):** `businesses.banner_url`, `banner_original_url`, `banner_crop` (https only, the crop shape check of product photos, original and crop only with a banner). Writable by exactly whoever writes `logo_url`: the row policies are unchanged, a column update grant is added and the three columns are added to the owner columns of `guard_business_admin_fields`. Readable by a visitor as `banner_url` only (column grant), and only for an active company (the existing row policy). The site-content gate (`private.validate_site_content`) checks `chrome.catalogBanner` and `get_published_site_content('chrome')` returns it; no other check changes. Rollback file and PGlite tests are part of the change.
- **Storage:** the company's files go to the public bucket `business-logos` under the company's own folder (`{business_id}/{uuid}.{ext}`), the same place and policies as its logo. The bucket's limit goes from 2 MB to 40 MB, the limit of `site-content`, because the untouched original of a banner is stored there and the app accepts pictures up to 5 MB (`IMAGE_RULES`); a 2 MB limit would refuse a normal photograph. The logo's own limit (2 MB) is held by the app (`LOGO_RULES`) and does not change. Saving a changed banner removes the old copy and original that nothing uses any more, only inside the company's own folder; deleting a company already sweeps its folder.

## Consequences
- Good: a catalogue can look like its company, and the platform can set the look of every other one, without a developer.
- Good: no new dependency, table, role or screen; the crop dialog, frames, Edit picture, site-content gate and company form are reused.
- Good: nothing new is exposed to visitors: the banner picture is public like the logo; the original and the crop need a signed-in reader. (The original file sits in a public bucket and can be opened by anyone who has its address, as the logo can; the address is not served to visitors.)
- Trade-off: the public bucket now holds originals of up to 40 MB under the company folders; a company folder is swept when the company is deleted, and a replaced banner's files are removed, but a failed clean-up leaves a file behind.
- Trade-off: until the site content is read, a catalogue without its own banner shows a plain dark band for a moment (one shared read, the same one the footer uses).
- Trade-off: the 2:1 crop is trimmed by the band on a phone (about a third of the width is out of view); the editors say so.
- Trade-off: a company being created has no banner field until it is saved.

## Alternatives rejected
- Store the company banner in site content: the document is the platform's and published as a whole; a company's picture belongs to the company's profile, with its permission and its deletion.
- A new table or bucket for banners: the logo's bucket, policies, folder rule and clean-up already do exactly this.
- Keep the 2 MB bucket limit and store only the cropped copy: Edit picture would restart from the already-trimmed copy, unlike every other picture (ADR-0063).
- Stage a banner under `new/` while a company is created: two files and a crop to move and re-point, for a step done once; the company is created first, the banner next.
- Let the banner replace the texts too ("Both"): the owner chose "Picture only"; the name, logo and count are the catalogue's identity and stay automatic.
