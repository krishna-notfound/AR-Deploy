# 16 · Analytics
Read when: building overview stats, AnalyticsView, charts, or seed events. Load the `dataviz` skill before writing chart code.

**Scope:** product views and AR activations **only**. No date-range pickers, device breakdowns, monthly reports, or 3D-interaction tracking.

## Data
- **Seed:** 30 days of daily events per published product, generated by a seeded PRNG so the numbers are the same on every load. These events have `sample: true`.
- **Live demo:** visiting a ProductPage and clicking AR record real events through `recordEvent`, with `sample: false`.
- **Aggregation:** the mock service computes `AnalyticsSummary` (see [04-domain-model.md](04-domain-model.md)). The UI only renders the result.

## AnalyticsView({ businessId? })
- **Header:** business filter (super admin only) and a "Sample data" tag.
- **StatCards:** views, AR activations, AR rate.
- **Line or area chart:** views and AR activations over the last 30 days.
- **Admin only:** bar chart of views and AR by business.
- **Top products** list.
- **Product performance table:** product, business, views, AR, AR rate. Sortable.
