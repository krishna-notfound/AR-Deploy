- use the site for anything unlawful, or misuse the report channel (C1).

## A3. 3D models and AR: what to expect

> **In short:** a good preview, not a measuring tape.

- **AR shows how a product might look in your space. It is a visual aid, not a measurement.** Colour, texture and apparent size can differ depending on your screen, the lighting and how well your phone tracks the room.
- A model marked **"True-to-size model"** matches the dimensions listed by the business within 5% (or 1 cm for small pieces). On iPhone, the separate AR file may not have been checked, and the product page says so. A model marked **"AR scale unverified"** may not appear at real size.
- **Before you buy, measure your space and confirm dimensions, colours and materials with the store.**
- **Stay safe:** keep aware of your surroundings while using AR, don't use it while walking near hazards or driving, and supervise children.
- AR opens in your phone's own viewer (Google Scene Viewer on Android, Apple Quick Look on iPhone and iPad). We don't control those apps, and whether AR is available depends on your device. Their providers' terms apply.

This section informs you; it does not limit the business's legal duties to you or your rights as a consumer.
