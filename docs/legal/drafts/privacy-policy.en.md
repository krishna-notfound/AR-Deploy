## 3. What we collect and why

> **In short:** for visitors, almost nothing and nothing that names you. For applicants and account holders, what we need to review the application and run the account securely.

The *legal basis* is the rule in the GDPR (Art. 6(1)) that allows each use: **(b)** contract, or steps you ask for before a contract; **(c)** legal obligation; **(f)** our legitimate interests, which we name each time.

### 3.1 Visitors

| Data | Why | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Your IP address and browser details, as part of every web request | To deliver the pages and files you ask for | (f) running the service |
| A short-lived key made from your IP address, a secret and today's date (for IPv6, only the network part of the address). It changes every day. | To stop abuse of our statistics endpoint (at most 30 events per minute) | (f) security of the service |
| An event saying "product X was viewed", "AR was started" or "the 3D model was used", with the time. **No identifier for you is stored with it.** | Product statistics shown to the business and to us, as totals | (f) letting businesses and us see which products interest people |
| Preferences kept in your own browser (section 5) | Remember your language and dismissed tips | Not personal data we receive |

The daily key is *pseudonymised* data, not anonymous: we hold the secret used to make it. We use it only to limit requests and delete it within an hour (section 8). We do not count unique visitors, build profiles or follow you across websites.

### 3.2 Applicants

| Data | Why | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Business name, website, country, city, category, language; your name, email, phone (optional) and role; your answers about the business and its catalog | To review the application and contact you about it | (b) steps you ask for before a contract |
| A key made from your IP address and a secret | To limit the form to 5 submissions per hour from the same connection | (f) preventing spam and abuse |
| Our decision, the reason for it, who reviewed it and when | To record and explain the decision | (b), and (f) being able to show how we decided |

Fields marked as required are needed to review the application; without them we can't consider it. People on our team make every decision; nothing is decided automatically. Your email address appears in the notification our team receives about new applications.

### 3.4 Data we get from someone else

- **Invitations:** the Sanctus team enters a future owner's email; the platform owner enters a future team member's email and name. We use them only to send the invitation and set up the account.
- **Business contact details:** a business enters its contact email, phone, address and website. If these identify a person, see section 4.

### 3.5 Emails we send

We send only service emails: application confirmations and decisions, invitations, security codes and notices, closure messages and business notices. We keep a log of each email: recipient address, subject, delivery status, any provider error, and the business or application it relates to. We keep it to prove what was sent and whether it arrived (legal basis (f)). Our emails carry no open or click tracking.

## 5. Cookies and device storage

> **In short:** we set no cookies. Your browser keeps a few small items so the site works; the table lists all of them.

| Item | Kept in | What it holds | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sign-in session (`sb-…-auth-token`) | Local storage | Your sign-in tokens (user id, email, sign-in level) | Keeps account holders signed in | Until you sign out or the session expires; it survives closing the browser |
| `arfurn:sign-in-pending` | Session storage | A flag | Records your sign-in once | Until you close the tab |
| `arfurn:invitePasswordSaved` | Session storage | Your user id | Completes an invitation | Until you close the tab |
| `sanctus:apply:draft` | Session storage | Your unsent application (name, email, phone, answers) | So you don't lose your answers | Deleted when you submit; otherwise when you close the tab |
| `arfurn:lang` | Session storage | `en` or `pt` | Your language choice | Until you close the tab |
| `arfurn:arTipsDismissed` | Local storage | `1` | Hides AR tips you dismissed | Until you clear site data |
| `arfurn:inAppNoticeDismissed` | Session storage | `1` | Hides the in-app browser notice you dismissed | Until you close the tab |
| `arfurn:sidebar` | Local storage | `0` or `1` | Remembers a collapsed dashboard menu | Until you clear site data |
| `arfurn:reloadedForNewBuild` | Session storage | A time | Prevents a reload loop after an update | Until you close the tab |

When you view a product, open its 3D model or start AR, the page sends our server a short message with the product and the event type (section 3.1). It uses no cookie and stores nothing on your device. You can clear all of the items above in your browser settings at any time.

## 6. Who receives data

> **In short:** three service providers that work on our instructions, plus Google or Apple when you start AR on your phone.

**Our providers (*processors*)**, bound by data-processing agreements:

| Provider | What they do for us | Where the data is | Safeguard for transfers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supabase | Database, sign-in, file storage, server functions | EU (Ireland). Support, account and service telemetry may be handled from Singapore or the US. | Standard contractual clauses |
| Vercel Inc. | Hosting and delivering the website | Global network; US company | EU-US Data Privacy Framework, plus standard contractual clauses |
| Resend | Sending service emails | United States | EU-US Data Privacy Framework, plus standard contractual clauses |

**Others:**

- **AR on your phone.** When you start AR, your phone's own viewer opens: Google Scene Viewer on Android, Apple Quick Look on iPhone and iPad. On Android, the model's web address is handed to Google's app, which downloads the model; Google's terms and privacy policy apply. Quick Look runs on your device.
- **Businesses** see only totals (views, AR launches, 3D interactions) for their own products. They never see who you are.
- **Authorities**, when the law requires us to share data.

We do not sell or share personal information for advertising. Our pages load their fonts and 3D files from our own servers, not from third parties.

## 9. Your rights

| Right | What it means |

| Restriction | Ask us to pause use of your data while an issue is resolved |
| Portability | Receive the data you gave us in a common, machine-readable format |
| Objection | Object to uses based on our legitimate interests (sections 3.1, 3.3, 3.5); we stop unless we have compelling grounds or need it for a legal claim |

**How:** email [[PRIVACY_EMAIL]] or write to [[ADDRESS]]. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We reply within one month; for complex requests we may extend this by two months and will tell you why. Requests are free unless they are clearly unfounded or excessive.

**Visitors:** we store no identifier for you, so we usually can't find events that relate to you. Your daily key is deleted within an hour.

## 10. Complaints

> **In short:** talk to us first if you like, but you can always go to the CNPD.

You may complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the **Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD)**, at [www.cnpd.pt](https://www.cnpd.pt/cidadaos/participacoes/), or to the authority of the EU country where you live or work.

## 11. Security

> **In short:** sensible protections, no absolute promises.

Our database is hosted in the EU. Connections are encrypted. Access is controlled row by row, so each account sees only what its role allows. Account holders can use two-factor authentication; security codes are stored only as salted hashes. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your data at risk, we will notify the CNPD and, where the law requires, you (GDPR arts. 33–34).
